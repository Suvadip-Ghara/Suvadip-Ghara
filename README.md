## About me

I am an **MFSc student in Fish Biotechnology** and a bioinformatics-focused researcher working at the intersection of **aquaculture, transcriptomics, artificial intelligence, and molecular biotechnology**. I enjoy building research databases, analysis interfaces, and decision-support tools that make biological data easier to explore and apply.

- 🧬 Exploring transcriptomics, gene expression, functional annotation, and fish genomics
- 🐟 Applying biotechnology and computation to aquaculture and fish health
- 🤖 Developing practical AI and data-driven tools for biological research
- 🧪 Interested in CRISPR, RNAi, and molecular approaches in aquaculture
- 📊 Focused on clear interfaces, reproducible workflows, and useful research outputs

## Featured projects

### [CST-Hub — *Channa striata* Transcriptomics Database](https://github.com/Suvadip-Ghara/Transcriptomics_Database)
A searchable resource for RNA-seq-derived gene expression data, DEG analysis, functional annotation, pathways, and supporting statistics.

### [Aquaculture Tools](https://github.com/Suvadip-Ghara/Aquaculture_Tools)
A practical web application concept for water quality, fish management, feed planning, monitoring, and aquaculture decision support.

### [CIFA Project](https://github.com/Suvadip-Ghara/CIFA-Project)
A web interface for murrel gene annotation, SSR markers, differential expression, functional annotation, and biological pathways.

### [Himalayan AquaTech](https://github.com/Suvadip-Ghara/Himalayan-AquaTech)
An AI-enabled cold-water fisheries concept focused on farmer advisory, fish health, climate awareness, and expert support.
